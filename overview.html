How a farm's carbon footprint is produced

From a farm's year of records to a finished Carbon Footprint report for DAERA, in six steps. This page explains what happens at each step, who is involved, and what happens when something isn't quite right.

Illustrative only. No real farm data is shown.

Who does what

Four parties each have one clear job.

LMC

Brings together each farm's records for the year and sends them in when they're ready.

Sans Souci platform

Checks, prepares and tracks every farm's data, and assembles the finished report. It never calculates emissions itself.

Farm Carbon Toolkit

The independent calculator. Every emissions figure in the report comes from here.

DAERA

Receives the finished Carbon Footprint for each farm, for DOLS and for analytics.

    When something isn't quite right

    Most farms go straight through. When one doesn't, it is paused and the right person is told. Nothing is guessed to keep it moving.

    Paused, not failed

    Something is missing or unclear

    If information is missing, or doesn't match anything the calculator recognises, the farm is paused. The status says exactly what is needed and who needs to act: usually LMC, sometimes us.

    Nothing is overwritten

    Corrections start a new version

    Once the information is fixed, it is sent in again and runs as a new version. The earlier version is kept exactly as it was, so there is always a clear record of what changed and when.

    Checked before every big step

    Nothing goes out unchecked

    Before the farm goes to the calculator, before the report is produced, and before anything is sent to DAERA, the platform confirms it is safe to go ahead. If not, the farm waits.

    What you can rely on

    These principles run through every step.

    • No invented figuresEvery emissions number comes from the Farm Carbon Toolkit. The platform never calculates, estimates or fills in a figure.
    • "Not available" never means zeroIf a figure couldn't be produced, the report says so and explains why, rather than showing a misleading zero.
    • Every number can be tracedEach value in the report can be followed back to the farm record it came from and the calculator answer it produced.
    • Farm identity kept to a minimumThe platform works from an anonymous farm reference. It never needs a farm's name, address or postcode.
    • People decide the hard casesAnything missing, conflicting, duplicated or uncertain goes to a person to resolve, not to an automatic guess.
    • Progress is always visibleLMC can check where any farm is at any time, and if it's paused, why.
