Where a case can land — the lifecycle, with the non-happy exits marked

A submission moves left to right. Two states are reachable from anywhere: held (waits on a person or the operator posture) and failed (the pipeline could not finish). Two more close the case at the end: completed and completed_with_warnings.

the walk accepted→ canonicalising→ validating_readiness→ planning→ calculating→ reconciling→ calculated→ generating_output
the landings completed — outputs released completed_with_warnings — outputs + approved findings held — waits on a person failed — terminal, nothing released

Every landing is a named state carrying a reason and a blocker_owner — never a stack trace, unless the failure is a genuine transport fault.

Held — waiting on a person, not a retry

A hold is a named refusal: the pipeline stopped at a specific stage for a specific reason, and wrote down who can clear it. The case row shows held and it lands on the operator's attention list (GET /ops/cases/attention) with its reason and findings.

Data problem blocker_owner: LMC

Something in the payload can't be prepared: an unmapped code, a missing field, an identifier that isn't a GUID. LMC fixes the dataset and resubmits under a new submission id — the new trigger is adopted onto the same case, which is re-assessed where it held:

  • fixed → it continues to the next stage;
  • still an issue → it holds again — same reason, or a different one.

Posture problem blocker_owner: SANS_SOUCI

Nothing is wrong with the payload — the estate's control gate is paused. The case waits at the gate before the next irreversible step (dispatch, render or release): no calculator calls are spent, nothing is released, and when the posture flips the case resumes at that gate.

state: held reason: <named refusal> blocker_owner: LMC | SANS_SOUCI ops/cases/attention

Completed with warnings — done, and flagged

The pipeline finished and the output pack was released — but not everything was clean. completed_with_warnings is still a deliverable state: GET /results answers 200 with the artefacts plus a warnings array of approved, non-blocking findings.

Typical warning: a final provider reply that was not a success. It is recorded in the evidence — not retried forever, and not severe enough to hold the case — and it rides along with the outputs so a reviewer can see exactly what was imperfect.

terminal + deliverable GET /results → 200 + warnings[]

Failed — terminal; nothing was released

A refusal with no human blocker: the pipeline could not complete. No output pack exists for DAERA — the failure is recorded on the case and in the operational journal, and it appears on the attention list.

Recovery is a correction, not a rerun. The failed version stays exactly as it was — it is the immutable record of what happened. A further submission under a new submission_id opens a new version of the farm-year's case: linked to its predecessor by supersedes / superseded_by, rendering its outputs under v{n}/ artefact paths so nothing written before is overwritten.

"A corrected farm-year submission creates a new immutable case version; it does not overwrite released evidence." — the published contract.
state: failed terminal supersedes → v1 superseded_by ← v2 {case}/v{n}/…

Refused at the door — synchronous; no case was opened

These answers come back from POST /api/v1/sanssouci/carbonfootprints itself — before any orchestration exists. Success is always 202 (accepted for async processing), never 200.

statusscenariocodewhat to do
400 The body isn't valid JSON. SSOS-SCHEMA-000 Fix the body and resubmit.
401 The auth posture refuses by default (deny_all). SSOS-AUTH-001 Sort out credentials / posture, then retry.
409 submission_id reused with a different payload — two bodies claiming one identity. SSOS-IDEM-002 Replay the original bytes, or submit under a new id.
409 The stored evidence already holds different bytes for this identity, and no case exists to adjudicate. SSOS-EVIDENCE-001 Reconcile which body is authoritative; resubmit with the right identity.
409 The case write was refused and no surviving record can be found (a pathological race). SSOS-IDEM-003 Resubmit; escalate if it repeats.
422 A prohibited identity field is present (e.g. a postcode). Paths only — nothing is stored. SSOS-IDENTITY-001 Remove the field and resubmit.
422 Contract validation failed. The staged evidence is kept, but no trigger is written and nothing runs. SSOS-SCHEMA-* Fix the findings and resubmit.
500 A genuine transport fault mid-write — storage or SQL unreachable. — Retry; nothing was falsely accepted.
503 The app couldn't build persistence at startup — every route reports it. — Platform-side; check GET /health.

Replays & retries — safe by construction

  • Same submission id + same logical payload → 202 with idempotent_replay: true. Nothing is re-staged, no trigger blob is written, no orchestration starts, no second FCT call is spent — the response just hands back the original carbon_footprint_id.
  • Same submission id + different payload → 409. That is a different submission claiming an identity that already has evidence — refused, never overwritten.
  • New submission id, same farm-year → the case it belongs to decides: a live (non-terminal) case is adopted and re-assessed; a terminal case opens a new version.
